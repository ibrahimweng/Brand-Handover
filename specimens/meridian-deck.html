The system

Everything is a fraction of r

A 12 unit square, a ring of radius 5, a stroke of 0.9. The tide line is an arc of 3r crossing a quarter of a radius below the centre.

Store one number and the whole mark rebuilds. Store four and they drift apart.

The system

Two marks, one rule

The divider is arithmetic on the clear space you already set in 1.3. The gap either side is x, the rule is the cap height of the wordmark, and nothing here is a judgement.

The partner mark is scaled to matched optical area, never matched height. A wide wordmark and a compact monogram set to the same height look nothing alike, and that is the mistake this rule exists to stop.

The system

One rule, three densities

The repeat is tied to the size of the thing it sits on, so a card and a hoarding carry the same visual weight rather than the same measurement.

Fine · under A5

Medium · A5 to A1

Coarse · above A1

Seamless in both directions, so it cuts to any shape without a join. Beacon appears only on Deep, because on Chalk it measures 1.8:1.

24
The system

Icons inherit the mark

Three numbers carry across, and they are why the set looks like it belongs here.

The margin1 in 12 for the mark, 2 in 24 for an icon. Same fraction.
The stroke0.9 in a 12 box becomes 1.8 in a 24 box.
The curve1.25 times the box, everywhere. In the mark that is 150. In an icon it is 30.
25
Set once

You draw them. It keeps them.

The system exports every size a platform asks for, and refuses an icon whose stroke, cap or curve radius breaks the rule. That refusal is the whole value, because a set falls apart at the twentieth icon rather than the eighth.

31
The system

The part that is arithmetic

The direction cannot be automated. The measuring can, and it is the thing people actually get wrong.

The system reads the pixels under the mark, works out the real contrast and raises a Deep scrim until it clears 4.5:1. On a bright sky that lands near 65 percent. A layout that fails is not published.

Voice

34
Yours

Say the number

Meridian sells predictability, so a vague claim undoes the product. Never claim the future. No weather metaphors, ever.

Say

The lagoon produced 41 MW on average across 2030.

Not

The lagoon delivered outstanding performance last year.

Say

We have not built at this depth before.

Not

We are pioneering unprecedented depths.
35
The system

Seven flags, five of them certain

MERIDIAN'S Estuary Programme Delivered Outstanding Results In 2030. The lagoon utilised four turbines to generate 41 megawatts on average, and we will revolutionise coastal power by 03/04/31.

The name, the case, the lexicon, the unit and the date format are exact, because they are string matching. Two flags are marked as a judgement, because the checker can find the word and cannot tell you whether what replaces it is any good.

The estuary programme produced 41 MW on average across 2030. The next site is scheduled for 3 April 2031.

The system

Three series, then stop

A five colour palette does not make six chart series. Inventing a sixth hue is where brand systems go wrong, so the rule refuses instead.

Tide carries the dataOne series, and the default for any single measure.
#1E7A8C
Slate carries the comparisonLast year, the forecast, the benchmark. Always recessive.
#5C6B70
Beacon marks one valueThe peak, the breach, the thing being discussed. Never a series.
#F2A007

Past three series, facet into small multiples. Labels are set in Deep or Slate and never in the series colour, which follows from the contrast work in 2.3 rather than from taste.
