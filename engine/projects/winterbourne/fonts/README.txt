Where these came from
=====================

Liberation Serif and Liberation Sans, SIL Open Font Licence 1.1, subsetted to
the 96 glyphs a brand document actually sets — latin, figures, the punctuation
this engine's own copy uses, and the currency and dashes a client will type.
Full faces are around 400 KB each; these are about 17 KB, which is what makes
it reasonable to inline them in every document.

They stand here for a licensed typeface. The mechanism is identical for a
commercial licence and only the terms differ, which is the point of the
"licence" field on the family in project.json: a client handed webfonts needs
to know what they may do with them, and that is the half of a typeface which
is not in the file.
