Print inks — optional, and the one thing the engine cannot work out for you

A hex value describes light leaving a screen. What it becomes in ink depends on a press and a paper no formula knows about, so the engine says so rather than inventing four numbers. Ask the printer, or read them off a swatch book, and the logo PDFs come out in those inks.
